Privacy policy

This policy explains what the Academy CRM website does with information you give it.

Contact form

The form asks for your name, email address, and message. The site uses them to show your confirmation on the page. It does not save the message in a database, and it does not send it by email.

Cookies

Sending the form needs a CSRF cookie. That cookie checks the form came from this site. It is not used for advertising.

Fonts

Pages load the Prompt font from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google to download the font files.

Questions

Questions about this policy can be sent from the contact page. The terms and conditions cover use of the site.
